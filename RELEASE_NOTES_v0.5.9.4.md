# PocketBridge 0.5.9.4

- Removes the fixed 16 MB app upload cap. Files are streamed to disk; available phone storage and the local connection determine whether a transfer can finish. A 15 GB movie is supported when the phone has enough free space.
- Shows shared files in Chat with a recipient confirmation before download/open.
- Adds Music with song search, shuffle, previous/next and automatic next-track playback.
- Adds file size filters and a remembered list/grid layout. Includes documents and APKs in Files.
- Centers video playback and adds fullscreen sizing, 10-second skip controls and aspect-ratio choices.
- Keeps screen capture running when the laptop viewer connects, avoiding a capture restart at that point.

Transfers use the phone's internal available space. Incomplete files are removed after storage or connection failures. Download the APK before going offline; after installation, the local hotspot transfer features need no internet connection.
