## PocketBridge 0.5.9.8

- Moved laptop lock, wake display, unlock/sign-in, and shutdown controls to Connect.
- Added laptop screen status reporting and a JPEG fallback for browsers without MediaSource support.
- Added Play in VLC for video and audio files in the browser library and chat attachments.
- Added Copy for chat text and Play/View actions for shared media.
- Reset video playback to the original aspect ratio and preserved the full frame in fullscreen.
